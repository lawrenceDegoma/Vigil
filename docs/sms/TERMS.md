# Vigil Alerts: SMS Terms and Conditions

**Program:** Vigil Alerts, a private, non-commercial monitoring system run by an individual for their own household.

## What you will receive
- **Urgent alerts:** a text when the home camera detects **possible** seizure-like movement in the household's pet, plus up to two reminder texts if nobody acknowledges the alert.
- **Early warnings:** a text when the system detects **possible** vomiting.
- **Device notices (account owner only):** a text if the monitoring device goes offline, and when it comes back online.

Alerts are automated observations, not a diagnosis, and they may be wrong. They never contain marketing, promotions or advertising.

## Who can receive these messages
Only the account owner and members of their household who have personally agreed to receive alerts. See [how consent is collected](OPT_IN.md). Numbers are never bought, rented or collected from the public.

## Message frequency
Varies, and depends on what the camera detects. Usually 0–10 messages per month. One event can produce up to 3 messages (an alert and two reminders).

## Costs
**Message and data rates may apply**, depending on your mobile plan. Vigil Alerts never charges recipients.

## Opting out and help
- Reply **STOP** to stop receiving messages. You will get one confirmation and no further messages.
- Reply **START** to receive messages again.
- Reply **HELP** for help, or contact the account owner who added your number.

## Other terms
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- Delivery depends on mobile networks, phone settings and internet service at the monitored home, so it is not guaranteed. Do not rely on Vigil Alerts as your only way of looking after an animal or person.
- Handling of your phone number: see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
