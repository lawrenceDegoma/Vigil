# Vigil Alerts: Privacy Policy

Vigil is a private, non-commercial home monitoring system run by an individual for their own household. This policy covers the people who receive Vigil Alerts text messages and use the Vigil dashboard.

## What is collected
- **Mobile phone number:** used only to send the alerts described in the [SMS Terms](TERMS.md).
- **Name or display name and sign-in email:** only for household members who have a dashboard account.
- **Alert records:** the type and time of each detected event, which messages were sent, and who acknowledged them.
- **Event video clips:** short clips recorded only when an alert is raised, showing the monitored area of the home. Ordinary footage stays on the home device and is never uploaded.

## How it is used
Only to run the monitoring and alerting service: sending alerts and reminders, showing incidents to household members, and keeping the system working.

## Sharing
- **No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.**
- **Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.**
- Phone numbers and personal data are never sold or rented.
- Data is processed only by the service providers that run the system: the messaging provider (Twilio), which delivers texts, and the database and storage provider (Supabase), which hosts alert records and clips. Each uses the data only to provide its service.

## Retention
- **Phone numbers:** kept until you opt out (reply STOP) or ask to be removed.
- **Uploaded event clips:** deleted after 30 days unless the account owner chooses to keep a specific clip.
- **Message delivery records:** kept as an audit trail while the household account exists.

## Security
- Access requires a personal sign-in, and each household can see only its own data.
- Video clips are stored privately and can be viewed only through short-lived links.
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by the hosting providers. This is not end-to-end encryption.

## Your choices
- Reply **STOP** to any message to stop texts.
- Ask the account owner to remove your number, account or data at any time.
