Home pet-monitoring alerts for one household

Vigil is a private, non-commercial monitoring system. A camera at home watches the household's dog and, when it sees possible seizure-like movement or possible vomiting, sends a text alert to the members of the household.

Operator: Lawrence Alfred Garcia Degoma (sole proprietor), doing business as Vigil Alerts

Contact: vigilalerts.contact@gmail.com

Country: United States

How it works

  • Video is analysed on a small computer inside the home. Ordinary footage never leaves the home.
  • When the system detects a possible event, it records a short clip and alerts the household.
  • Household members can view the clip and acknowledge the alert through a private, sign-in-only dashboard.
  • The system is an experimental monitoring aid. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

The Vigil Alerts text program

  • Who receives texts: only the operator and members of the operator's household (no more than 5 people), each of whom has agreed in person. There is no public sign-up.
  • What is sent: alerts about possible events, up to two reminders if an urgent alert is not acknowledged, and offline notices to the operator only. Never marketing.
  • Frequency: varies with what the camera detects; usually 0–10 messages per month.
  • Costs: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. Reply HELP for help.
