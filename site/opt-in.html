Vigil Alerts: how consent (opt-in) is collected

Opt-in type: verbal, in person. Vigil Alerts has a few recipients: the operator, Lawrence Alfred Garcia Degoma, and members of their own household (no more than 5). There is no public sign-up, web form or keyword, and numbers are never obtained from lists or third parties.

Process

  1. Ask in person. Before adding anyone, the operator explains the program using the script below and asks whether the person wants to receive alerts.
  2. Agreement. The person's number is added only if they clearly say yes.
  3. Record. The operator enters the number in the private Vigil admin console, which time-stamps the entry. Adding a number is restricted to the household administrator and is written to an audit log.
  4. Ongoing control. Recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out (handled automatically, with a single confirmation), START to opt back in, or HELP for help.

Consent script (read to each recipient)

"I'd like to add your mobile number to Vigil Alerts, our home pet-monitoring system. You'd get a text if the camera sees possible seizure-like movement or possible vomiting in our pet, up to two reminders if nobody responds, and nothing else: no marketing. It's usually 0 to 10 texts a month. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop them, or HELP for help. The terms and privacy policy are at lawrencedegoma.github.io/Vigil. Do you agree to receive these texts?"

Sample messages

Vigil URGENT: possible seizure-like movement seen for [pet name] at 9:41 PM (Living room). Please check now.
Vigil reminder 1/2: possible seizure-like movement for [pet name] at 9:41 PM has not been acknowledged.
Vigil: possible vomiting seen for [pet name] at 9:41 PM (Living room).
Vigil: Living room is OFFLINE. No heartbeat since 9:30 PM. Monitoring and alerts may not be working.
